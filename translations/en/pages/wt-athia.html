Goddess of Wisdom.

  1. To start her content complete Gwen's story up to the point of the ritual to summon her. Afterwards talk to Gwen in her cottage again about allying. This should then start the quest the next time that you leave the cottage.
  2. Visit the church in Arenfield during the day.
  3. Talk to Athia outside of the church.
  4. Give her a day to cool off then talk to her again, she won't see reason. At midnight Harvald will visit her at the church, interact with him there.
  5. The next day check on them again, you now have the choice to interfere or stay out. If you don't act Harvald will make a move on her (interfering will also drastically increase her affection for you). If you interfered or not, the next time you talk to her there is now the option to invite her into your house.
  6. You now have to renovate your house up to the 1st floor (see house renovation).
  7. Now visit Roderic and ask him to renovate a room for Athia. After that is done talk to her to show her the new room.
  8. She is still running around in rags, time to change that by visiting Frisha. Ask her for a new dress and pay 10 gold.
  9. Return home and talk to Athia in her room.

Depending on your choice with Gwen there are 2 paths going forward.

Dominating Athia (If you choose to help Gwen)

  1. Continue to raise your affection with Athia.
  2. If you haven't done it already ask Roderic about the bathtub and complete it (again house renovation).
  3. You now have to build affection with her, you can do that by giving her food (bread or other cooked meals) or if you built the kitchen eat with her at 4pm and praise her.
  4. After reaching 10 affection with Athia join her in the bathroom at 6pm.
  5. The next day talk to her again in her room.
  6. Talk to her again in her room and resist; you need 15 affection.
  7. Have another bath with her.
  8. Talk to her inside her bedroom the next day.
  9. After that all actions can be replayed by either taking a bath or talking to her in her room / spying on her at midnight.
  10. After having sex with Athia and a week has passed, You will feel a summon to Gwen, visit her in her cottage and talk to her near the cauldron.
  11. Return home and invite Athia to your bedroom (interact with the bed).

Helping Athia (If you decline to help Gwen)

  1. Go to Gwen's house, check the bookshelf in the main room after Gwen has gone to bed. After that you can now continue to raise your affection with Athia.
  2. If you haven't done it already ask Roderic about the bathtub and complete it (again house renovation).
  3. You now have to build affection with her, you can do that by giving her food (bread or other cooked meals) or if you built the kitchen eat with her at 4pm and praise her.
  4. After reaching 10 affection with Athia join her in the bathroom at 6pm.
  5. The next day talk to her again in her room.
  6. Lockpick the door when she is playing with herself (right after the dialogue or after midnight); you need 15 affection.
  7. Have another bath with her.
  8. Spy on her again when she is masturbating then go inside.
  9. After that all actions can be replayed by either taking a bath or talking to her in her room / spying on her at midnight.
  10. Talk to her in the morning at 9am when she's on her bed reading. This will trigger a weekly timer after which she has found information (alternatively you can complete her route up to step 18 to instantly access this). The timer is only meant for people who don't want to do any sexual content with her. This will now start a new quest around the church. There isn't much to discover yet, it will be continued in the future.
  11. For the next step you need to continue Mira's quest up to the point where she undergoes the ritual in the sanctum underneath the church or until you completed the quest around the Monastery and either imprisoned Syfa or made a pact with her. If you decide to banish Syfa then this quest can't be continued for now, and you will have to wait until Mira's next update.
  12. Tell Athia about the demon underneath the church, and bring her there. Depending on choices Athia will accept the bond with Syfa either on her terms or on Syfa's. After this Athia will be able to get corrupted and to further progress with her you will need to have sex with her in her room.
  13. Invite Athia to your room in the house.

The next step hasn't fully been implemented, but you can already have sex with Athia inside the bathroom and upstairs when she is praying.
